## The Product

*What it does, for whom, current focus.*

**Streakly** — consumer habit + micro-learning app. Users pick a track and do a short (5-minute) daily lesson to build a streak; the streak is the core habit loop. Launched 4 years ago, Series B funded ($42M), 2.1M registered users, 340K MAU, growing 28% YoY on MAU.

**Core/north-star metric:** Day-7 retention (product-wide, not just this initiative).

**Current phase:** Discovery (Day 1 — 2026-09-28), investigating root cause of the Comeback problem below.

**Current focus — the Comeback problem:** Day-7 retention slipped from 48% to 39% after the streak v2 redesign shipped. The drop is sharpest among users who break their streak in week 1 — churn roughly doubles after two consecutive missed days.

**Key tension:** the streak mechanic that drives engagement (loss aversion) is the same mechanic that punishes users when it breaks — a cold reset to zero, a harsh "you lost your streak" push notification, and no acknowledgment or path back in.

**Open decision:** diagnose whether the root cause is the reset mechanic, the notification tone, or both — and evaluate whether the "Comeback screen" concept (best-streak stat + 60-second comeback lesson + one-tap streak-freeze) is the right direction. Forcing function is a Thursday alignment meeting.

**Stakeholders:** Marcus (driving the initiative), Raj (data/analytics), Lena (research/design, proposed the Comeback screen concept).
