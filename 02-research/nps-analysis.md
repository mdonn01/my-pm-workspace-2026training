# NPS Feedback Analysis — Streakly

*Source: 10 raw NPS comments. Shared for Marcus — 2026-09-28.*

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once

| Rank | Theme | Frequency | Representative Quotes |
|------|-------|-----------|------------------------|
| 1 | All-or-nothing streak reset, no way to recover | 7 of 10 | "I hit a 20-day streak, missed one day, and it reset to zero. I haven't opened the app since." / "The streak is the only thing keeping me engaged, but the second I lost it, I was done." / "I broke my streak once and there was no way to recover it. Other apps let you freeze a streak." / "The home screen looks the same whether I'm on a 2-day streak or coming back after two weeks away." |
| 2 | Notification strategy is misfiring (too naggy for some, not compelling enough for others) | 3 of 10 | "The daily reminder just started to feel like nagging." / "I got three [notifications] in one afternoon and just turned them all off." / "I forget it exists after a couple of days. If it pulled me back with something useful I'd come back." |
| 3 | Lesson content itself is well-liked (praise) | 2 of 10 | "The first week was genuinely fun." / "Love the lessons." |

*Note: the home-screen comment above was a single, standalone mention but is folded into Theme 1 since it describes the same comeback moment.*

## Praise vs. Complaints

| Type | Summary | Count |
|------|---------|-------|
| Praise | Lesson content is enjoyable | 2 of 10 |
| Complaint | Reset mechanic, no recovery path, notification tone/frequency, lack of home-screen acknowledgment, "scorekeeper not coach" framing | 8 of 10 |

## Top 3 Actionable Issues

| Priority | Issue | Why it matters |
|----------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | No recovery mechanism after a streak break | Most-cited driver of stated disengagement/deletion; directly supports the existing "Comeback screen"/streak-freeze hypothesis in strategy.md |
| 2 | Home screen doesn't reflect a user's actual state (short streak vs. returning after time away) | Concrete UI gap, distinct from the reset mechanic itself |
| 3 | Notification strategy needs retuning, not just fewer notifications | Some users are over-notified and disabling reminders; others feel forgotten and want a more useful pull-back — a blanket volume fix would help one group and hurt the other |
